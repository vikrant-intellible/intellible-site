How it works

We work through your churned accounts one at a time. For each one, we check whether it can be contacted and who it is today, find the real reason it left and look for what has changed since. Before anyone calls, the pitch is tested against the people who will decide. What comes back is a ranked list of who to call this week, why now and what to open with. There is more on the method in what is win-back intelligence.

Who it is for

Commercial, marketing and customer success teams at B2B companies who want to know why accounts churned and which ones are worth winning back. Each team uses the intelligence differently: commercial teams get a ranked list of who to call this week, why now and how to open the conversation. Marketing teams see the real reasons accounts left, so messaging speaks to what has changed since. Customer success teams learn which problems drove the churn, so they can fix them and look after a returning account properly.

Where we work

We work with B2B companies across the UAE, KSA, the GCC and the EU. Our focus is B2B SaaS, fintech, edtech payments, foodtech and cloud kitchen operators.

Who runs Intellible

Intellible was founded in Dubai in August 2026 by Vikrant Sharma, after nine years in operations and customer experience across MENA and India at Zomato, Uber Eats, Careem, CloudKitchens and EasyOrder. He started Intellible after building a more accurate churn predictor and asking whether the same thinking could show which already-churned accounts were worth winning back.

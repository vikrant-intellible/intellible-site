A ten-minute exercise

The lost
accounts audit

Ten minutes, five questions, one churned list.

Pull every churned and closed-lost account from the last 24 months in your CRM, pick five, and run each through the questions below.

01 / 05

Why did they really leave?

Not the dropdown reason; if it says price, check their last login.

02 / 05

Has that reason expired?

The champion left, the missing feature shipped, the budget reset.

03 / 05

What has happened to them since?

New funding, new leadership, hiring or expansion.

04 / 05

Who would you call, and would they take it?

05 / 05

Whose job is this?
